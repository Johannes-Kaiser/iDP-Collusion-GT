# Polish C6 / C7 / C8 (K=200) locus figures

## Context

The three canonical K=200 locus figures were auto-regenerated by Slurm
job 173474 via `game_experiments/run_locus.py`, but the resulting PDFs
were unreadable at print scale:

- **fig_C6_locus_K200 (left panel)**: BR curves were rendered as `o-`
  markers on the raw discrete-argmax indices, producing single-pixel
  spikes wherever the K=200 argmax tied between neighbouring cells; and
  all 48 pure-NE stars were plotted individually, forming an illegible
  black cloud on the diagonal.
- **fig_C7_w_ratio**: 241 NE points across six weights were drawn as
  translucent Blues-palette dots, colors bleeding into each other.
- **fig_C8_asymmetric_w**: same problem with 172 NE points across five
  ratios and the Oranges palette.

## Inputs (unchanged)

Real caches under `game_experiments/_cache_v2/`:

- `C6_K200.pkl` -- 48 pure NEs, BR indices, u1 surface, K=200 grid.
- `C7_K200.pkl` -- six weights `w in {0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4}`,
  `|NE|` in `{35, 40, 48, 48, 52, 18}`.
- `C8_K200.pkl` -- five ratios `w_1/w_2 in {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4}`,
  `|NE|` in `{32, 37, 48, 35, 30}`.

No experiment re-run required.

## Changes

### fig_C6_locus_K200 (two panels)

- **Panel (a): smoothed BR loci + clustered NE stars.**
  Best-response curves are now rendered as running-median-smoothed
  lines (window=7) on `log10(actions[BR_i(j)])` as a function of
  `log10(actions[j])`. The median filter fuses single-pixel argmax
  jumps into the local monotone envelope without distorting the
  monotone envelope. NEs are grid-clustered in log10 eps space with
  a 0.15-decade cell (roughly 7 K=200 grid steps, matching what a
  reader perceives as "one NE" at print scale): the 48 raw NEs
  collapse to 28 cluster centroids plotted as star markers whose size
  scales weakly with per-cluster count.
- **Panel (b): NE density heatmap.**
  Replaces the u1 surface (which duplicated Corollary C1 already
  shown by fig_C1). A 24x24 log-eps 2D histogram of NE locations is
  rendered in viridis, with the smoothed BR curves and the same
  cluster centroids overlaid in white for reference. Log-decade
  ticks are labelled in raw eps.

### fig_C7_w_ratio (single panel)

- Perceptually uniform `viridis` palette, six discrete color stops
  spanning `w in {0.125, ..., 4}`.
- Per-weight rendering: (1) 68% Mahalanobis dispersion ellipse
  computed on the log-log NE cloud, filled at alpha=0.15, so overlap
  is visible without color-bleeding; (2) cluster centroids (0.15
  decade cells) as filled circles; (3) a bold `+` at the overall
  log-log centroid to summarise the position at a glance.
- Legend moved to two columns, log-log axes framed to
  `[0.5, 64]^2`, diagonal reference kept.

### fig_C8_asymmetric_w (single panel)

- Same layout as C7 with the `cividis` palette (color-vision-safe,
  distinct from C7 by design) mapped to `log2(w_1/w_2)`, so
  `w_1/w_2 = 1` sits near the middle of the ramp and asymmetric
  ratios are ordered around it. Markers are squares (matches the
  legacy scheme) with cross overlays at the overall centroid.

### Style-match with `fig_h8_brpeg_regret`

- Sizes via `game.style.two_column_size(rows=1, aspect=0.42)` for C6
  and `column_size(aspect=0.85)` for C7/C8, matching the h8 aspect.
- Panel titles use the `(a)/(b)` prefix convention of h8.
- All figures share `apply_paper_style()` (grid on, top/right spines
  off, savefig.dpi=300).

## Reproducibility

`polish_C6_C7_C8.py` is deterministic: it loads pickles and runs
`scipy.ndimage.median_filter` and simple grid clustering. To regenerate
from scratch after a cache invalidation, first re-run
`game_experiments/run_locus.py` (via `slurm_scripts/run_locus.sh` per
Rule 3) and then re-run the polish script.
