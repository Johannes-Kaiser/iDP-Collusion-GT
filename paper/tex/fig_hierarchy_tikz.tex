\caption{\textbf{The advantage hierarchy at two contract points}
(top: $\eps = 10$, $\delta = 10^{-12}$, the paper's canonical
setting; bottom: $\eps = 2$, $\delta = 10^{-12}$). Six
Poisson-subsampled Gaussian mechanisms (blues), each calibrated by
exact PLD accounting so that its privacy profile passes through the
row's contract point (orange dot). \emph{(a)} Away from the shared
point, the profiles differ by orders of magnitude. \emph{(b)} The
exact trade-off curves $T(\alpha)$~\cite{dong2022gaussian}; the thin
diagonal is no leakage, the dotted orange envelope $f_{\eps,\delta}$
the mechanism-agnostic worst case. \emph{(c)} The realised advantages
$A_{\mathrm{PLD}}$ sit far below the ceiling $A_{\eps,\delta}$
(orange bar): up to $0.50$ versus ${\approx}1$ at the top contract,
$0.12$ versus $0.76$ at the bottom. The contract alone neither
determines nor tightly bounds the realised risk.}
\label{fig:hierarchy}
